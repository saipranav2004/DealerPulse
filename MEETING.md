# Meeting notes: DealerPulse walkthrough

Target: 8 minutes of talking. Let him interrupt. Answers are below.

---

## 1. Open with this (20 seconds)

"I built it around one idea. A CEO has ninety seconds, so the screen should
tell you what is wrong, not make you find it."

Then read the top line off the screen.

> Lakeside converts 7.6 percent of its leads where the group converts 31.4
> percent, and the break is at the very first step. 42 percent of Lakeside
> leads are never called.

"That sentence is not typed. The code compares every branch, finds the worst
one, walks its funnel, and writes the line. Different data next month gives a
different line, or it tells you there is nothing worth saying."

---

## 2. Show three screens only (4 minutes)

**Overview.** Point at the two money boxes.
"Fixing the phone calls is worth about 40 to 56 lakh. Fixing the whole funnel
is worth 3.95 to 5.5 crore. I keep them apart because they are very different
sized jobs and mixing them would have overstated the easy one."

Scroll to Branch comparison.
"Every rate carries its count. Lakeside is 6 of 79. Sixty percent of five and
sixty percent of five hundred should never look the same."

**Act now.** 
"This is the to do list. 8.59 crore is sitting in 38 orders where the customer
already paid and no car was delivered. Normal is 17 days. The oldest is 195.
30 of the 38 are already past the date your own rep promised."

Tap "Why this order?"
"The ranking is a formula I show on screen. Money already committed outranks
money still being discussed."

**Vehicles.**
"Glanza pulls 130 enquiries and earns 3.97 crore. Fortuner pulls 94 and earns
12.61. Where the floor spends its time is a choice."

---

## 3. Three decisions (2 minutes)

**Money as a range, not one number.**
"Those 38 stalled orders. If your team chases them most complete. If nobody
does, many will not. I do not know which, so I show both ends and say what
separates them."

**It refuses to answer sometimes.**
"Under ten leads it will not print a rate. Under thirty it will not name a
branch as the problem. A branch too small to judge is also too small to clear,
so I withhold the whole finding rather than blame whoever happens to be big."

**A quarter has two right answers.**
Switch the basis button on Q4.
"24.81 crore is what you delivered in those months. 17.55 crore is how the
leads you took in those months converted. Both are correct. Most dashboards
silently pick one. I let you switch and I label it."

---

## 4. What I chose not to build (30 seconds)

Say this. It matters more than the feature list.

"I dropped a row spacing control after I measured it. It saved twelve percent
of height and added a button that looked like a menu. I also skipped a what
changed since last visit marker, because your data is a fixed export and it
would have been fake."

---

## 5. Close (30 seconds)

"Three things next. Point it at a live source and unfreeze the clock. Let
people share saved views with each other. And sit with one branch manager for
an hour to see which screen they open second. I have a guess. I would rather
know."

---

## If he asks

**How do you know the numbers are right?**
"33 checks recompute every headline figure from the raw file and compare it to
what the app shows. Different code on each side, so a bug that moves both the
same way still fails."

**How do you know it works on a phone?**
"A second suite opens a real browser over 32 views at four widths in both
themes. It caught a two pixel decoration I added that was pushing every table
row one column across from its own header. Invisible on a laptop. On a phone it
put the customer name over the value column."

**Biggest weakness?**
"It reads a file, not a live system. And the target data is unusable. The plan
asks for 1,426 units when only 510 leads exist, so I show attainment for
continuity and keep it out of every health signal."

**How long did it take?** Be honest with your real number.

**Did you use AI?**
Be straight. "Yes, like most engineers now. The judgment calls, the checks and
the things I removed are mine, and I can walk you through any line of it."
Do not deny it. Getting caught in that is worse than the question.
